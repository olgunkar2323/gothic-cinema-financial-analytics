# Gothic Horror Movies: Financial Performance & Distribution Analytics

A well-rounded SQL-based data analytics work exploring the financial parameters, distribution types, risk profiles and secondary revenue dependencies across 23 Gothic Horror movies (1992-2025).

This project shapes with **database querying**, **financial modeling (ROI, Net Profit etc.)**, and **business dynamics** to obtain strategic insights for movie studios and entertainment producers.

## Executive Summary
- **Theatrical vs. Streaming Dynamics:** Theatrical releases reach remarkably higher average ROI (**175.19%**) and average net profit (**$92.73M**) compared to Streaming releases (**60.66% ROI / $12.59M profit**). However, Streaming offers predictable risk mitigation and steady viewer retention (Avg **Retention Score: 8.8/10**).

- **The "Secondary Revenue" Rescue Effect:** Secondary revenues (VOD, streaming rights, TV, merchandise) constitute a **25.93% share** of total theatrical revenues, turning box-office underperformers (e.g., *Crimson Peak*, *I, Frankenstein*) into net profitable assets.

- **Capital Efficiency & Risk Profiles:** High Risk / High Reward Theatrical projects absorbed 75% of total capital investments ($1.19B) while generating **90.5% of total net profits ($1.20B)** across the table.
